Notes for a first-time Indian founder

Short, practical pieces on what trips people up early. Not legal advice, just a clearer starting point.

Patents

Is a software idea patentable in India?

Section 3(k) of the Patents Act, 1970 excludes “a computer programme per se”. Software with a real technical effect, or tied to specific hardware, can still qualify. It depends on how the claim is written.

Patents

File a provisional first

A provisional application is cheaper and locks in your priority date. You then have 12 months to file the complete specification while you keep building.

Funding

DPIIT recognition and the SIPP scheme

DPIIT-recognised startups can use the Startup Intellectual Property Protection scheme to reduce patent filing costs. Check your eligibility before paying full price.

Collaboration

Why manual approval beats auto-matching

A matching algorithm sees skills and availability. It cannot tell who will still show up when the idea gets hard. Reading each application yourself keeps that judgment with you.

Building

What to ask your AI co-founder

Give it real constraints: budget, timeline, and who your first ten users are. Specific questions get specific answers.

Building

Stay private until the brief is sharp

Publish once the problem statement is something you would happily hand a stranger. Until then, private costs you nothing.
